# Partnerships Content Expansion Project

## Executive Summary

This project helps the Editorial Partnerships team turn high-performing MSN slideshows into new article opportunities with minimal incremental editorial effort. The workflow uses AI to identify the strongest existing slideshow winners, then recommends a small set of peel concepts the team can assign quickly and confidently.

## Why It Matters

MSN’s revenue-sharing changes make standard articles more valuable than before. That creates an opportunity to expand proven slideshow content into additional article formats, increase the volume of high-performing partner stories, and grow syndication revenue without relying on new reporting.

## How It Works

Each week, the system reviews Business Insider slideshows that have earned more than 90,000 pageviews on MSN in the last 7 days. It then generates three article concepts based on the topics, framing, and audience signals that performed best, giving the Partnerships team a focused set of ideas to assign.

## Success Metric

The goal is a measurable increase in high-performing MSN articles and incremental revenue from the MSN partnership.
